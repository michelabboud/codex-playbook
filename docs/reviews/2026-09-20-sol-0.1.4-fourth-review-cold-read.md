## Cold-read findings

### 1. Blocking — carried open batches have no line-local state, breaking the claimed equivalence

- **A:** `.agents/skills/codex-playbook-reviews/SKILL.md:45-46`, worked case 7 at line 58
- **B:** `rules/REVIEWS.md:51-52`, worked case 7 at line 64

The rule says a batch has one of three states, says one batch is open per line, and says a branch cut from an open batch carries that batch while new work opens another batch on the branch. It never says what state that carried batch has on a later merged line.

Counterexample: line X opens A; lines Y and Z branch from A and open B and C; Y and Z each merge back into X while A is still open, with both merge commits belonging to A. X now carries `{A, B, C}`. When A closes, X has no locally open batch, but B and C remain open on their owning lines. The supposedly equivalent test, "a new batch starts only while at most two closed batches are unruled," sees only one closed batch on X and admits D, producing four unruled ranges. Treating B and C as closed snapshots on X would avoid that result, but the text neither says that nor pins review targets for those snapshots. Treating their global state as open instead leaves more than one open batch reachable from X.

This is not merely terminology: the invariant and its stated equivalent yield different admission results. Define batch state per line, including the exact state and review obligation of an inherited or merged snapshot, or forbid merging an open source batch. If foreign open work becomes an inherited snapshot on the destination, say that it occupies a closed/unruled slot there and specify which pinned target reviews it.

### 2. Blocking — a fix can be attached after the review target is pinned without any required review of the fix

- **A:** `.agents/skills/codex-playbook-reviews/SKILL.md:45`, worked cases 9 and 16 at lines 60 and 67
- **B:** `rules/REVIEWS.md:51`, worked cases 9 and 16 at lines 66 and 73

Closing freezes membership and pins the deep-review target. The exception then lets a finding-fix land attached to that closed batch. Ruling requires the blocker to be fixed or dismissed, but no sentence makes the fix commit itself an input to a mechanical review, a focused deep re-review, or a renewed pinned target. A literal coordinator can therefore accept a defective fix and mark the batch ruled after checking only that the original finding no longer reproduces.

Keep the original target immutable, record attached fix commits separately, and require a pinned focused review of every attached fix before ruling. Say who validates that review and what happens if the fix introduces a new finding.

### 4. Blocking — work with no plan-allocated batch is unlandable

- **A:** `.agents/skills/codex-playbook-reviews/SKILL.md:45`
- **B:** `rules/REVIEWS.md:51`

Admission happens only at the first dispatch of "a task the plan allocates to it." A fresh line can admit its first planned batch correctly, but a single hotfix, a small docs correction, or other authorized one-task change for which no plan is required has no allocatable batch. The rule simultaneously forbids landing outside a batch. Requiring a new plan would contradict the authority layer's exemption for ordinary single-task work.

Permit the coordinator to admit an explicitly identified ad-hoc one-task batch when no approved plan is required; record it in the same ledger and give it the same task and batch review obligations.

## State-machine walk

- **Count 0, no open batch:** the first planned batch admits and moves to count 1. Unplanned work is blocked by finding 4.
- **Count 1, open:** tasks land in it; closing leaves the count at 1 and pins review. A second batch cannot open concurrently on that line.
- **Count 1, closed:** a second batch may admit; a ruling drops the count to 0; a finding-fix may attach, but finding 2 leaves its review obligation undefined.
- **Count 2, one open:** it may keep building or close; closing leaves two closed.
- **Count 2, none open:** a third batch may admit and must be announced.
- **Count 3, one open:** it may keep building or close; no fourth batch may admit.
- **Count 3, none open:** only finding-fixes may land; ordinary merges wait. The fix-review hole remains.
- **Early close under rule 3.2:** row 14 now correctly preserves the straggler and moves it to the next batch, which cannot land until that batch is admitted.
- **Fresh branch from open A:** row 7 correctly charges A immediately and requires new work to open a new batch. Later merge-back is not fully defined because the carried open state is not made line-local (finding 1).
- **Merge into an open destination batch:** assigning the merge commit and conflict resolution to that open destination batch is sound when its eventual pinned target includes the merge. Merging an open source batch remains underspecified.
- **Gate:** lower reviews settle and the candidate freezes correctly, but row 12 omits the separate line's required batch admission.

The sentence "a new batch starts only while at most two closed batches are unruled" is exact only if every carried batch that does not own the destination line's open slot is defined as closed on that destination. The current text does not define that, so the equivalence does not hold in all allowed merge states.
